DrawingML preset shape definitions
=================================

resources/presetShapeDefinitions.xml is the shape definition resource
distributed by Apache POI 5.4.1 (release tag REL_5_4_1), with mixed CRLF/LF
line endings normalized to LF. XML content is unchanged.

Figure Editor evaluates numeric guides and paths from this fixed resource.
It does not execute XML, load remote resources, or evaluate source code.
The preset resource's circularArrow, leftCircularArrow and
leftRightCircularArrow definitions contain formulas with surplus operands.
Those definitions are rejected rather than silently repaired.
